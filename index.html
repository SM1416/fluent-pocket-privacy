Fluent Pocket: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Fluent Pocket is an English learning app published by Mallouk 1416. This policy explains what information the app handles.

The short version

The app collects no data at all. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data, and it works fully offline.

Information stored on your device

Your learning progress (finished lessons and your Premium status inside the app) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves your device, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Speech

Lessons are read aloud by your device's own built-in text-to-speech service. The app passes the lesson text to that service on your device so it can be spoken. Nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Premium is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive, see, or store your payment information. Your purchase is tied to your Google account, and reinstalling the app with the same account restores Premium.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It contains no advertising and no in-app chat or user-generated content.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
